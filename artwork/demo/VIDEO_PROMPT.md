# Prompt: the SpaceBook Library app demo video (English + Urdu)

Use this prompt to recreate or adapt the video with an AI video tool, an editor, or Claude.

---

**Make two promo / demo videos of the SpaceBook Android app: one with English narration, one with Urdu narration. Same footage, same design, different voice and captions.**

**About the app.** SpaceBook is a study app for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa textbooks, Classes 1 to 12. The Library tab shows every class and each class's real textbook covers. A book opens page by page, looking like the printed page. Tapping a sentence shows its meaning in Urdu and Pashto, with a speaker to read it aloud; holding a word shows just that word's meaning (Urdu, Pashto, Arabic). Exercises have interactive activities. Maths answers show the whole step-by-step solution at once, or as an animated worked solution on a board. The app is **coming soon** (do not claim it is released; do not claim games are ready).

**Format.** 1920x1080, 30 fps, MP4 (H.264 + AAC stereo, 48 kHz), about 2.5 to 3 minutes. Deep-violet "space" background with soft stars; accent colours violet, pink, gold, mint. Friendly, bright, kid-safe, fast-paced but clear.

**Screen layout (after the hook).** Left: a real phone screen recording inside a rounded phone frame (clean status bar, 10:00, full battery, no notifications). Right: a panel with the "SpaceBook Library" brand at the top, a gold badge naming the step, and the spoken line shown as a large caption. Class scenes show the class illustration card, a huge class number, "N books", and a row of subject chips. Maths scenes show the expression and each result in a large maths font; the final answer in big green.

**Structure and timing (English cut, about 2:33).**
1. **Hook, 0 to 4 s.** A tilted 3D wall of real book covers scrolling past; riser sound; four giant words slam in one at a time with a thump, a pulsing ring and a small sub-line, each said aloud as it lands: READ (every lesson), LISTEN (every sentence aloud), SOLVE (step by step), PRACTISE (tests and quizzes). White flash.
2. **Logo, 4 to 9.7 s.** "SpaceBook" in big glowing letters, "LIBRARY" underneath, tagline "Every textbook, Class 1 to 12, in one app."
3. **Library tour, about 58 s.** Open the Library tab, scroll the covers, then tap the class chips 1 to 12 in turn. For each class: 3 to 5 seconds of voice, the class card and subject chips on the right, the phone showing that class's covers. Footage played up to 1.6x faster for pace.
4. **Class 1 English, about 27 s.** Open the English book, choose Unit 2 "My Family and I", open the Exercise tab. Tap a sentence (meaning panel in Urdu and Pashto), press the speaker, hold the word "letter". Page one: match pictures to letter sounds (tap picture, then letter; success sparkle). Page two: choose the right word for the gap (a wrong try says "try again", then the right word jumps in with confetti).
5. **Class 6 Mathematics, about 43 s.** Chapter 3, Laws of Integers and Order of Operations. Exercises tab, Exercise 3.7 (page arrows in the bottom corners turn pages), question 1 part (e): 725 - [20 x {10 + 7 + (7 x (5 - 3))}]. Tap Ans: the whole step-by-step solution appears at once. Tap Animate: the board solves it step by step: 5 - 3 = 2; 7 x 2 = 14; 10 + 7 + 14 = 31; 20 x 31 = 620; 725 - 620 = 105 (fanfare when 105 appears).
6. **Ending, about 6.5 s.** SpaceBook logo, huge "COMING SOON", "Read - Learn - Play", chime.

**Sound design.** Voice always clearest. Cheerful background tune (C - G - Am - F, about 100 bpm: soft pad, marimba/kalimba arpeggio, light bass, quiet shaker) that dips under the voice. Sound effects: riser and impact thumps in the hook, big whoosh and sparkle on the logo flash, soft swish when a new caption appears, whoosh between clips, tap clicks on every touch, a pop when a popup opens, sparkle on a correct match/answer, a soft "bonk" on the wrong try, a short fanfare when 105 appears, bell chimes for "coming soon". Final loudness about -16 LUFS, peaks under -1.5 dB.

**Rules.** No personal data or other apps on the phone screen; keep the Urdu text right to left; keep every spoken line fully audible (hold the last frame or slow the footage slightly if a line is longer than the action); end on "coming soon".
